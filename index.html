Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my Tonvyn Pro subscription? Open Tonvyn → My Capsule → tap the gear icon (Studio Settings & Privacy) → tap Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Paywall).
  • How do I cancel my subscription or free trial? Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. Open iOS Settings → [Your Name / Apple ID] → Subscriptions → Tonvyn to modify or cancel anytime.
  • How do I delete all my face calibration and closet data? Open Tonvyn → My Capsule → Studio Settings & Privacy → tap Delete All Face & Closet Data for an immediate, irreversible wipe.
